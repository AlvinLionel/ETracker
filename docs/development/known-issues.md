## Confirmed defects and risks

| ID | Area | Finding and impact | Evidence / next action | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KI-002 | Forecast scheduling | Scheduled generation runs when enabled settings are saved and during app startup. It uses a unique UTC `scheduled_month`, so retries that month are idempotent. If the app misses a month, next startup generates only for the current month; it does not backfill missed months. | `src-tauri/src/persistence/forecasting.rs`, `generate_scheduled_if_due`; behavior is explicitly approved. | Documented limitation |
| KI-004 | Source-file privacy | Expired source-file BLOBs are deleted at startup with SQLite `secure_delete=ON`, followed by a best-effort WAL truncate checkpoint. This reduces recoverable copies in SQLite pages and the WAL, but cannot guarantee physical erasure on filesystems, snapshots, backups or SSD media. Original files are limited to 25 MiB and XLSX expansion to 100 MiB. PDF parser memory behavior and local database encryption at rest remain open. | `src-tauri/src/infrastructure/database/connection.rs`, `migrations/0005_import_source_files.sql`; a startup cleanup test checks expired-row deletion, secure-delete mode and WAL truncation. Review deletion expectations before release (`DB-032`). | Partially addressed — open security/privacy verification |
| KI-005 | Diagnostics | Settings can download a redacted support report with app/platform versions and database health status; detailed integrity output remains local in Settings. Persistent structured logs and a retention policy are not implemented, and some Tauri commands still translate persistence failures to generic user errors. | `src-tauri/src/persistence/diagnostics.rs`, `src-tauri/src/persistence/integrity.rs`; persistent logging and retention remain pending (`PEND-028`). | Partially implemented — open |

## Resolved items removed from the old outstanding list

The earlier entries for planning, analytics and forecasting were stale: budgets, savings, subscriptions, analytics, local forecasts and forecast evaluation now exist and have tests. OW-010 is resolved: budget plans load into the template editor; account-list CSV/Excel rows are reviewed in Accounts and safely create new KES accounts, with account numbers ignored, duplicate pairs and unsupported currencies excluded. Tests cover dated balances and classification of dated account lists versus statements. KI-003 and Q-002/Q-008 are resolved by `DB-033`: linked refunds appear in both their own and their original expense's audit histories, with receipt, void and reversal recorded atomically. These are not open issues; broader release verification remains.
